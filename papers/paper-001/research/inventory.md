## Scope and screening

Three parallel researcher searches covered self-consistency, independent verification, and evidence-based fact-checking. Requirements: 1,200-word scholarly demonstration, APA 7, peer-reviewed sources, at least three relevant publications, and limitations/opposing perspectives. The suggested papers were evaluated against publisher metadata, venue and relevance criteria rather than accepted automatically. No additional rubric or publication-date cutoff was supplied.

11 catalogued candidate records represent 11 unique publications; all 11 pass research screening and have accessible publisher full text. There are 0 cross-report duplicates, 0 unresolved identity matches, and 0 rejected catalogued candidates. DOI matching came first, followed by canonical URL and title/author/year comparison. The ICLR paper uses its proceedings URL for canonical identity; its arXiv DOI is not a conference DOI. Mirror/preprint search hits were collapsed by researchers before candidate registration. Off-topic search hits were not onboarded. Raw candidate count therefore means catalogued records, not every search hit.

Acceptance establishes suitability for later reading, not verified findings, methodological superiority, final citation selection, or human approval. This bounded search is not systematic or exhaustive.
