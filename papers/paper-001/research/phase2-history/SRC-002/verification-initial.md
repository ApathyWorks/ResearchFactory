Independent inspection of Tables 1-5 supports this limited observation, but it lacks a dedicated evidence-row mapping. Before using it as a manuscript claim, add it to the relevant table-result qualification or a new stable claim ID; do not generalize it to absence of all uncertainty analysis. This ancillary source-inspection note is not a major untracked research finding.
- The unspecified biography sample-size note is correctly restricted to the inspected task description (Section 4.1.4), rather than asserting that no sample size could exist elsewhere. It is an access/extraction uncertainty, not a numerical result.
- All major narrative methodological and empirical assertions otherwise map to C001-C012. Additional observations are clearly labeled interpretation cautions.

## 5. Material Omissions and Misinterpretations

No material omission makes the focused summary misleading. For any future comparison specifically asserting superiority over retrieval, the source contains an important boundary condition: the discussion accompanying Figure 2 says the retrieval-based comparator performs better on very rare facts (printed p. 3569, PDF p. 7). The present summary does not claim universal or retrieval-comparator superiority. If that comparison is later introduced, extract and verify a separate claim rather than infer it from aggregate Table 3 scores.
