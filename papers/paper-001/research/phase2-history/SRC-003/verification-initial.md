## 4. Untracked Narrative Assertions

- **U001 - Section 5, Key Finding 2:** The statement that the best PerplexityAI result comes from ChatGPT retrieval exceeds C005-C006 and is not true as an unqualified minimum-ER claim. Table 3 (printed p. 12082; PDF p. 7) gives Inst-LLaMA Retrieve-to-LM ER **0.1**, lower than ChatGPT Retrieve-to-LM **0.8**. The former does not preserve the ranking of all three generators; the latter does. Section 4.2 recommends ChatGPT retrieval for PerplexityAI in its comparison of two preferred variants. Replace the narrative with the narrower comparison or explicitly explain the ranking criterion. If adding the 0.1/ranking comparison as evidence, extend C006 while retaining its ID, or assign a new local ID and obtain verification; do not silently treat the current C006 as containing that comparison.

## 5. Material Omissions and Misinterpretations

1. **C007:** Appendix B.2 (printed p. 12092; PDF p. 17) states that human oracle atomic facts are supplied and that the metric targets Not-supported facts. This materially limits what the numeric comparison establishes about an end-to-end automatic factuality checker.
2. **U001:** Distinguish lowest ER for one generator from preservation of ranking across generators. Do not convert an author-recommended estimator into an absolute numeric winner.
3. **C013 implementation detail:** EditCorr discards gold edits with no new tokens (printed p. 12096; PDF p. 21). The current evidence row is otherwise supported and does not claim an all-example denominator; retain this detail when explaining the metric. This is a qualification for reuse, not a rejected claim.
4. The abstract refers loosely to 13 LMs, whereas Tables 4-5 and Figure 3 identify 12 LMs plus human text. The summary correctly follows the detailed account. The source also varies on cost-savings totals, and the summary appropriately avoids extracting a savings claim.
