# Research Paper Assignment

## Assignment Type

Demonstration academic research paper for evaluating
a multi-agent AI research and writing workflow.

This is a self-defined demonstration assignment.

## Working Title

Detecting and Reducing Hallucinations in Large Language
Models: A Review of Verification Techniques

## Research Question

How do self-consistency checking, independent verification,
and evidence-based fact-checking address hallucinations in
LLM-generated text, and what limitations remain?

## Paper Requirements

- Target length: 1,200 words.
- Use an academic writing style.
- Use APA 7th edition for citations and references.
- Use peer-reviewed scholarly publications.
- Include an introduction, analytical body, and conclusion.
- Compare findings across multiple publications.
- Discuss the strengths and weaknesses of each approach.
- Address limitations and opposing research findings.
- Support factual claims using verified scholarly evidence.

## Research Scope

The paper should investigate approaches for identifying
and reducing hallucinations in large language models.

Areas of interest include:

1. Self-consistency checking
2. Independent verification
3. Evidence-based fact-checking

## Research Expectations

- Identify at least three relevant scholarly publications.
- Prefer original peer-reviewed research.
- Evaluate conflicting evidence where available.
- Distinguish empirical findings from author interpretations.
- Identify methodological limitations.
- Avoid unsupported generalizations.
